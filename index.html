🧠 About Minds

There are two kinds of minds in the world today: biological and machine.

For example:

  • Biological minds are the brains of humans and animals.
  • Machine minds are AI systems — the most powerful of which are Transformers, like ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, or Grok.

The main job of every mind — whether biological or machine — is to learn and think.

And here’s the surprise: at their core, learning and thinking are just doing math. Yes, really! 😯

Math happens in every kind of mind, though through different means. When we build a machine mind, it learns on its own — so creating one feels less like assembling a LEGO house and more like training or nurturing a living thing.

How is all this even possible? In this course, you’ll discover the answer.

The Building Blocks of Every Mind

Every mind is, in essence, a neural network — — a team of tiny helpers called NEURONS that work together.

It’s called neural'' because it’s made of NEURONS.

😎
NEURON

It’s called a network because NEURONS connect to each other and work together.

Think of each NEURON as a small, smart helper.

But what does every NEURON do?

Actually, each one takes in input SIGNALS, does a little math, and sends out one output SIGNAL.

For example, a NEURON might take in two input SIGNALS, do a little math, and send out one output SIGNAL.

What’s a SIGNAL? Simply put, it’s anything that can be measured or calculated and turned into a number — like 0.36 or 0.53.

  • 1. Takes in input SIGNALS (e.g., 0.36 and 0.53)

    The Math Every Neuron Does

    When a mind thinks, every NEURON does very simple math.

    At its core, every NEURON works just like a store receipt.

    Analogy: RECEIPT

    Imagine buying donuts and cupcakes at a store:

    RECEIPT
    UNITS PRICE PER UNIT AMOUNT
    donut 4 2.00 8.00
    cupcake 2 3.00 6.00
    SUM 14.00
    DISCOUNT -0.50
    TOTAL 13.50

    The math is simple:

    TOTAL = 4.00 × 2.00 + 2.00 × 3.00 + (-0.50)
    TOTAL = 8.00 + 6.00 - 0.50
    TOTAL = 14.00 - 0.50
    TOTAL = 13.50

    This math can also be represented this way:

    4.002.00UNITS
    2.003.00PRICES
    8.006.00AMOUNTS
    14.00SUM
    -0.50DISCOUNT
    13.50TOTAL

    At its core, every RECEIPT is just a formula:

    TOTAL = UNITS1 × PRICE1 + UNITS2 × PRICE2 + DISCOUNT
    TOTAL = 4.00 × 2.00 + 2.00 × 3.00 + (-0.50)
    TOTAL = 8.00 + 6.00 - 0.50
    TOTAL = 14.00 - 0.50
    TOTAL = 13.50

    In terms of a NEURON, the same math can be represented this way:

    4.002.00INPUTS
    2.003.00WEIGHTS
    8.006.00PRODUCTS
    14.00SUM
    -0.50BIAS
    13.50OUTPUT

    At its core, every NEURON is also just a formula like this:

    OUTPUT = INPUT1 × WEIGHT1 + INPUT2 × WEIGHT2 + BIAS
    OUTPUT = 4.00 × 2.00 + 2.00 × 3.00 + (-0.50)
    OUTPUT = 8.00 + 6.00 - 0.50
    OUTPUT = 14.00 - 0.50
    OUTPUT = 13.50

    Ultra simple. No math changed. Only names changed.

    Importantly, in the case of RECEIPTS, when UNITS change, the TOTAL changes too:

    3.005.00UNITS
    2.003.00PRICES
    6.0015.00AMOUNTS
    21.00SUM
    -0.50DISCOUNT
    20.50TOTAL

    Similarly, in the case of NEURONS, when INPUTS change, the OUTPUT changes too:

    3.005.00INPUTS
    2.003.00WEIGHTS
    6.0015.00PRODUCTS
    21.00SUM
    -0.50BIAS
    20.50OUTPUT

    Again, no math changed. Only names changed.

    So every NEURON follows the same basic logic as a RECEIPT.

    The mapping:

    RECEIPT → NEURON
    UNITS→INPUTS
    PRICES→WEIGHTS
    AMOUNTS→PRODUCTS
    SUM→SUM
    DISCOUNT→BIAS
    TOTAL→OUTPUT

    The WEIGHTS and BIAS are called PARAMETERS.

    If you understand this, you already understand a lot of AI.

    By the way, every answer from ChatGPT ultimately comes from billions of tiny calculations that look surprisingly similar to the receipt example above.

    The most exciting part is how NEURONS themselves improve their PARAMETERS after making ERRORS. This process is called LEARNING.

    Scale: Billions and Trillions

    While today's largest AI models may each have over 1 trillion PARAMETERS, the human brain is vastly more complex.

    A typical human brain contains roughly 86 billion neurons with 500 trillion neural connections called synapses. You can think of each biological synapse as playing the role of a WEIGHT, while each biological neuron also has properties that loosely resemble a BIAS.

    Metaphorically speaking, a typical human brain has roughly 500.86 trillion "parameters" (500 trillion "weights" + 86 billion "biases").

    So every individual NEURON is relatively simple, but a large network of NEURONS can produce remarkably intelligent behavior.
