Introduction

In this class, for the first time, we are building a multilayer model — a 2-layer one.

(Here's a pic of the model.)

Also, we introduce two ACTIVATION FUNCTIONS — or ACTIVATIONS for short.

An ACTIVATION takes in a SUM and sends out a final number, which becomes the official OUTPUT of the NEURON.

Each ACTIVATION is simply a formula that behaves in an interesting way.

We consider two ACTIVATIONS: ReLU and SIGMOID.

ACTIVATION (ReLU)

Let's look at a famous ACTIVATION called ReLU.

ReLU's behavior is known as "SAVE or ZERO".

Imagine ReLU is a special math magician. Its favorite trick is a game called "SAVE or ZERO", which it plays with every SUM that passes through it.

When a SUM enters ReLU, the magician asks one simple question: "Are you positive or negative?"

  • SAVE (The Positives): If the SUM is positive (like 0.1, 0.7, or 10.0), ReLU SAVES it. The SUM passes through completely unchanged. So a 0.1 stays 0.1, and a 10.0 stays 10.0.
  • ZERO (The Negatives): If the SUM is negative (like −0.1, −0.7, or −10.0), ReLU ZEROS it, turning the SUM into 0.0. Only the 0.0 passes through. So a −0.1 becomes 0.0, and a −10.0 becomes 0.0.

In other words, ReLU takes in a SUM and sends out the maximum of that SUM and 0.0:

ReLU = max(SUM, 0.0)

INFLUENCE (ReLU)

Of course, when the ACTIVATION is ReLU, calculating the INFLUENCE of the SUM on the ACTIVATION is incredibly simple.

  • When the SUM is positive: The ACTIVATION = SUM. Therefore, the INFLUENCE of the SUM on the ACTIVATION is 1.0.
  • When the SUM is negative: The ACTIVATION = 0.0. Therefore, the INFLUENCE of the SUM on the ACTIVATION is 0.0.

In a NEURON, ReLU is both incredibly simple and incredibly useful. We will see this throughout this entire course.

ACTIVATION (SIGMOID)

Let's look at another famous ACTIVATION called SIGMOID.

SIGMOID's behavior is known as "ZERO TO ONE".

Imagine SIGMOID is another math magician. Its favorite trick is a game called "ZERO TO ONE", which it plays with every SUM that passes through it.

When a SUM arrives, SIGMOID doesn't ask questions. Instead, it squeezes that SUM — no matter how big or small — into a cozy new home between 0.0 and 1.0.

  • Huge positive numbers (like 10.0 or 100.0) get squeezed very close to 1.0 — but never quite reach it.
  • Huge negative numbers (like −10.0 or −100.0) get squeezed very close to 0.0 — but never quite reach it.
  • Zero (0.0) stays right in the middle — it becomes 0.5.

In other words, SIGMOID takes in any SUM and sends out a number that is always between 0.0 and 1.0.

SIGMOID = 1 / (1 + 1 / 2.71828SUM)

(Don't worry about the fancy formula — just know it's a gentle squeeze!)

INFLUENCE (SIGMOID)

When the ACTIVATION is SIGMOID, calculating the INFLUENCE of the SUM on the ACTIVATION is also incredibly simple.

  • When the SUM is near 0.0: The INFLUENCE is at its highest — around 0.25. That means a small change in the SUM makes a noticeable change in the ACTIVATION.
  • When the SUM is very positive or very negative: The INFLUENCE gets closer to 0.0. That means changing the SUM barely affects the ACTIVATION — it's already squeezed to its limit!

🧠 BRAIN

We're going to build a machine brain that can convert Celsius temperatures to Fahrenheit temperatures.

In this scenario, the INPUT represents Celsius and the OUTPUT represents Fahrenheit.

Let's call this brain the Converter-°C-to-°F.

As previously noted, it will have two PARAMETERS: WEIGHT and BIAS.

The brain will be just a formula like this:

OUTPUT = INPUT1 × WEIGHT1 + INPUT2 × WEIGHT3 + INPUT3 × WEIGHT3 + BIAS
Initial PARAMETERS

Suppose we don't yet know what these PARAMETERS should be, so let's start by setting them to zero:

WEIGHT = 0.0
BIAS = 0.0

At first, the brain will generate nonsense, since with any INPUT the OUTPUT is zero.

Dataset

We have this training DATASET consisting of two EXAMPLES:

# INPUT TARGET
EXAMPLE 1 0.0 32.0
EXAMPLE 2 1.0 33.8

EXAMPLE 1 means when the INPUT is 0.0, the OUTPUT must be 32.0, because 0.0°C equals 32.0°F.

EXAMPLE 2 means when the INPUT is 1.0, the OUTPUT must be 33.8, because 1.0°C equals 33.8°F.

During TRAINING, in each LESSON, the brain updates each PARAMETER so that the OUTPUT gets closer to the TARGET.

Update Rule

The brain updates each PARAMETER using the FUNDAMENTAL UPDATE RULE:

FUNDAMENTAL UPDATE RULE for PARAMETER
PARAMETER_update = ERROR × PARAMETER_OUTPUT_influence × PACE
PARAMETER = PARAMETER + PARAMETER_update

Because the brain has two PARAMETERS — WEIGHT and BIAS — we obtain these two update rules:

FUNDAMENTAL UPDATE RULE for WEIGHT
WEIGHT_update = ERROR × WEIGHT_OUTPUT_influence × PACE
WEIGHT = WEIGHT + WEIGHT_update
FUNDAMENTAL UPDATE RULE for BIAS
BIAS_update = ERROR × BIAS_OUTPUT_influence × PACE
BIAS = BIAS + BIAS_update

Don't worry — each step is simple. Just follow the calculations below.
