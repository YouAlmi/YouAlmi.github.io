Introduction

🎉 Big moment! For the first time, we're building a multilayer model — with 2 LAYERS!

Here's what our 2-layer model looks like. Cool, right? 😎

But wait — there's more! We're also introducing two ACTIVATIONS: ACT1 and ACT2.

What is any ACT?

Any ACT is just a formula.

What does an ACT do?

In a NEURON, an ACT takes in a SUM and transforms it into the final number, which becomes the official OUTPUT of the NEURON. Magic! ✨

So every ACT is simply a formula that behaves in an interesting way and has its own superpower.

Today, we meet two famous ACTS: ReLU and SIGMOID.

Let's dive in! 🚀

ACT (ReLU)

Let's look at a famous ACT called ReLU.

ReLU's behavior is known as "SAVE or ZERO".

Imagine ReLU is a special math magician. Its favorite trick is a game called "SAVE or ZERO", which it plays with every SUM that passes through it.

When a SUM enters ReLU, the magician asks one simple question: "Are you positive or negative?"

  • SAVE (The Positives): If the SUM is positive (like 0.1, 0.7, or 10.0), ReLU SAVES it. The SUM passes through completely unchanged. So a 0.1 stays 0.1, and a 10.0 stays 10.0.
  • ZERO (The Negatives): If the SUM is negative (like −0.1, −0.7, or −10.0), ReLU ZEROS it, turning the SUM to 0.0. Only the 0.0 passes through. So a −0.1 becomes 0.0, and a −10.0 becomes 0.0.

In other words, ReLU takes in a SUM and sends out the maximum of that SUM and 0.0.

The ReLU formula:

ReLU = max(SUM, 0.0)

Here is how we can write this "SAVE or ZERO" game in Python code:

🟢 GAME 1: SAVE ✨ The positive SUM is SAVED

Ready to execute!

🔵 GAME 2: ZERO 🪄 The negative SUM is turned into ZERO

Ready to execute!

So ReLU equals SUM — or becomes ZERO.

INFLUENCE (ReLU)

When the ACT is ReLU, calculating the INFLUENCE of SUM on ACT is incredibly simple:

  • When the SUM is positive: The ACT = SUM. Therefore, influence_SUM_ACT is 1.0.
  • When the SUM is negative: The ACT = 0.0. Therefore, influence_SUM_ACT is 0.0.

Therefore, when the ACT is ReLU:

influence_SUM_ACT = 1.0 (when SUM > 0.0)

influence_SUM_ACT = 0.0 (when SUM < 0.0)

influence_SUM_ACT = 0.0 (when SUM = 0.0)

Very easy!

ACT (SIGMOID)

Let's look at another famous ACT called SIGMOID.

SIGMOID's behavior is known as "BETWEEN ZERO AND ONE".

Imagine SIGMOID is another math magician. Its favorite trick is a game called "BETWEEN ZERO AND ONE", which it plays with every SUM that passes through it.

When a SUM arrives, SIGMOID doesn't ask questions. Instead, it squeezes that SUM — no matter how big or small — into a cozy new home between 0.0 and 1.0.

  • Huge positive numbers (like 10.0 or 100.0) get squeezed very close to 1.0 — but never quite reach it.
  • Huge negative numbers (like −10.0 or −100.0) get squeezed very close to 0.0 — but never quite reach it.
  • Zero (0.0) stays right in the middle — it becomes 0.5.

In other words, SIGMOID takes in any SUM and sends out a number that is always between 0.0 and 1.0.

The SIGMOID formula:

SIGMOID = 1 / (1 + 1/2.71828SUM)

Don't worry about this fancy formula — just know it's a gentle squeeze!

Here is how we can write this "ZERO TO ONE" game in Python code:

🟢 GAME 1: Almost ONE ✨ A huge positive SUM gets squeezed close to 1.0

Ready to execute!

🔵 GAME 2: Almost ZERO 🪄 A huge negative SUM gets squeezed close to 0.0

Ready to execute!

🟣 GAME 3: HALF 🎯 Zero (0.0) stays right in the middle: 0.5

Ready to execute!

So SIGMOID is gentle, smooth, and always keeps numbers between 0.0 and 1.0. It's like a calm teacher who never raises their voice too high or drops too low.

INFLUENCE (SIGMOID)

When the ACT is SIGMOID, calculating the INFLUENCE of SUM on ACT is also incredibly simple:

influence_SUM_ACT = ACT * (1 - ACT)

Very easy!

Some clever mathematicians figured out this super simple formula. 👍


  • When the SUM is near 0.0: influence_SUM_ACT is at its highest — around 0.25. That means a small change in the SUM makes a noticeable change in the ACT.
  • When the SUM is very positive or very negative: influence_SUM_ACT gets closer to 0.0. That means changing the SUM barely affects the ACT — it's already squeezed to its limit!

During TRAINING, in each LESSON, the brain changes each PARAMETER so that the OUTPUT gets closer to the TARGET.

Change Rule

The brain changes each PARAMETER using the FUNDAMENTAL CHANGE RULE:

FUNDAMENTAL CHANGE RULE for PARAMETER
PARAMETER_change = ERROR * influence_PARAMETER_OUTPUT * PACE
PARAMETER = PARAMETER + PARAMETER_change

Because the brain has multiple PARAMETERS — WEIGHTS and BIASES — we obtain these two change rules:

FUNDAMENTAL CHANGE RULE for WEIGHT
WEIGHT_change = ERROR * influence_WEIGHT_OUTPUT * PACE
WEIGHT = WEIGHT + WEIGHT_change
FUNDAMENTAL CHANGE RULE for BIAS
BIAS_change = ERROR * influence_BIAS_OUTPUT * PACE
BIAS = BIAS + BIAS_change

Don't worry — each step is simple. Just follow the calculations below.
