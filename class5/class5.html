Introduction

🎉 Big moment! For the first time, we're building a multilayer model — with 2 LAYERS!

Here's what our 2-layer model looks like. Cool, right? 😎

But wait — there's more! We're also introducing two ACTIVATIONS: ACT1 and ACT2.

What is an ACT?

Any ACT is simply a formula.

What does an ACT do?

In a NEURON, an ACT takes in a SUM and transforms it into the final number — which becomes that NEURON'S official RESULT. Magic! ✨

So every ACT is simply a formula that behaves in an interesting way and has its own superpower.

Today, we meet two famous ACTS: RELU and SIGMOID.

Here, RELU acts as ACT1 and SIGMOID acts as ACT2.

Let's dive in! 🚀

ACT (RELU)

Let's look at a famous ACT called RELU.

RELU's behavior is known as "SAVE or ZERO".

Imagine RELU is a special math magician. Its favorite trick is a game called "SAVE or ZERO", which it plays with every SUM1 that passes through it.

When a SUM1 enters RELU, the magician asks one simple question: "Are you positive or negative?"

  • SAVE (The Positives): If the SUM1 is positive (like 0.1, 0.7, or 10.0), RELU SAVES it. The SUM1 passes through completely unchanged. So, 0.1 stays 0.1, and 10.0 stays 10.0.
  • ZERO (The Negatives): If the SUM1 is negative (like −0.1, −0.7, or −10.0), RELU ZEROS it, turning the SUM1 into 0.0. Only the 0.0 passes through. So, −0.1 becomes 0.0, and −10.0 becomes 0.0.

In other words, RELU takes in a SUM1 and sends out the maximum of that SUM1 and 0.0.

The RELU formula:

RELU = max(SUM1, 0.0)

So RELU equals SUM — or becomes ZERO.

INFLUENCE (RELU)

When the ACT is RELU, calculating the INFLUENCE of SUM1 on ACT1 is incredibly simple:

  • When the SUM1 is positive: The ACT1 = 1SUM. Therefore, influence_SUM1_ACT1 is 1.0.
  • When the SUM1 is negative: The ACT1 = 0.0. Therefore, influence_SUM1_ACT1 is 0.0.

Therefore, when the ACT1 is RELU:

influence_SUM1_ACT1 = 1.0 (when SUM1 > 0.0)

influence_SUM1_ACT1 = 0.0 (when SUM1 < 0.0)

influence_SUM1_ACT1 = 0.0 (when SUM1 = 0.0)

Very easy!

ACT (SIGMOID)

Let's look at another famous ACT called SIGMOID.

SIGMOID's behavior is known as "BETWEEN ZERO AND ONE".

Imagine SIGMOID is another math magician. Its favorite trick is a game called "BETWEEN ZERO AND ONE", which it plays with every SUM2 that passes through it.

When a SUM2 arrives, SIGMOID doesn't ask questions. Instead, it squeezes that SUM2 — no matter how big or small — into a cozy new home between 0.0 and 1.0.

  • Huge positive numbers (like 10.0 or 100.0) get squeezed very close to 1.0 — but never quite reach it.
  • Huge negative numbers (like −10.0 or −100.0) get squeezed very close to 0.0 — but never quite reach it.
  • Zero (0.0) lands right in the middle — it becomes 0.5.

In other words, SIGMOID takes in any SUM2 and sends out a number that is always between 0.0 and 1.0.

The SIGMOID formula:

SIGMOID = 1 / (1 + 1/2.718SUM2)

Don't worry about this fancy formula — just know it's a gentle squeeze!

So SIGMOID is gentle, smooth, and always keeps numbers between 0.0 and 1.0. It's like a calm teacher who never raises their voice too high or drops too low.

INFLUENCE (SIGMOID)

When the ACT2 is SIGMOID, calculating the INFLUENCE of SUM2 on ACT2 is also incredibly simple:

influence_SUM2_ACT2 = ACT2 * (1 - ACT2)

Very easy!

Some very clever mathematicians figured out this super simple formula. 👍


Therefore, in the case of SIGMOID being the ACT2:

  • When the SUM2 is near 0.0: influence_SUM2_ACT2 is at its highest — around 0.25. That means a small change in the SUM2 makes a noticeable change in the ACT2.
  • When the SUM is very positive or very negative: influence_SUM2_ACT2 gets closer to 0.0. That means changing the SUM2 barely affects the ACT2 — it's already squeezed to its limit!

🧠 BRAIN

We're going to build a machine brain that can recommend whether you should go to a party or not.

In this scenario, each NEURON of LAYER1 receives three INPUTS at once.

These three INPUTS represent the three pieces of knowledge, and the OUTPUT represents the recommended action: GO or STAY.

If the OUTPUT is less than 0.5, it means GO. Otherwise, if the OUTPUT is 0.5 or greater, it means STAY.

Therefore, during inference, we'll round the OUTPUT — mapping 0 to GO and 1 to STAY.

Let's call this brain the Party-Recommender.

As previously noted, it will have multiple PARAMETERS: WEIGHTS and BIASES.

The brain will be just a formula.

During TRAINING, in each LESSON, the brain changes each PARAMETER so that the OUTPUT gets closer to the TARGET.

Change Rule

The brain changes each PARAMETER using the FUNDAMENTAL CHANGE RULE:

FUNDAMENTAL CHANGE RULE for PARAMETER
PARAMETER_change = ERROR * influence_PARAMETER_OUTPUT * PACE
PARAMETER = PARAMETER + PARAMETER_change

Because the brain has multiple PARAMETERS — WEIGHTS and BIASES — we obtain these two CHANGE RULES:

FUNDAMENTAL CHANGE RULE for WEIGHT
WEIGHT_change = ERROR * influence_WEIGHT_OUTPUT * PACE
WEIGHT = WEIGHT + WEIGHT_change
FUNDAMENTAL CHANGE RULE for BIAS
BIAS_change = ERROR * influence_BIAS_OUTPUT * PACE
BIAS = BIAS + BIAS_change

Don't worry — each step is simple. Just follow the calculations below.
