Also, we introduce two ACTIVATION FUNCTIONS — or ACTIVATIONS for short.

An ACTIVATION takes in a SUM and sends out a final number, which becomes the official OUTPUT of the NEURON.

Each ACTIVATION is simply a formula that behaves in an interesting way.

ACTIVATION

Let's look at a famous ACTIVATION called ReLU.

ReLU's behavior is known as "SAVE or ZERO".

Imagine ReLU is a special math magician. Its favorite trick is a game called "SAVE or ZERO", which it plays with every SUM that passes through it.

When a SUM enters ReLU, the magician asks one simple question: "Are you positive or negative?"

  • SAVE (The Positives): If the SUM is positive (like 0.1, 0.7, or 10.0), ReLU SAVES it. The SUM passes through completely unchanged. So a 0.1 stays 0.1, and a 10.0 stays 10.0.
  • ZERO (The Negatives): If the SUM is negative (like −0.1, −0.7, or −10.0), ReLU ZEROS it, turning the SUM into 0.0. Only the 0.0 passes through. So a −0.1 becomes 0.0, and a −10.0 becomes 0.0.

In other words, ReLU takes in a SUM and sends out the maximum of that SUM and 0.0:

max(SUM, 0.0)

INFLUENCE

Of course, when the ACTIVATION is ReLU, calculating the INFLUENCE of the SUM on the ACTIVATION is also incredibly simple.

  • When the SUM is positive: The ACTIVATION = SUM. Therefore, the INFLUENCE of the SUM on the ACTIVATION is 1.0.
  • When the SUM is negative: The ACTIVATION = 0.0. Therefore, the INFLUENCE of the SUM on the ACTIVATION is 0.0.

In a NEURON, ReLU is both incredibly simple and incredibly useful. We will see this throughout this entire course.

ACTIVATION

Let's look at another famous ACTIVATION called SIGMOID.

SIGMOID's behavior is known as "FROM ZERO to ONE".

Imagine SIGMOID is a friendly math genie. Its favorite trick is a game called "FROM ZERO to ONE", which it plays with every SUM that enters its lamp.

When a SUM arrives, SIGMOID doesn't ask questions. Instead, it squeezes that SUM — no matter how big or small — into a cozy new home between 0.0 and 1.0.

  • Huge positive numbers (like 10.0 or 100.0) get squeezed very close to 1.0 — but never quite reach it.
  • Huge negative numbers (like −10.0 or −100.0) get squeezed very close to 0.0 — but never quite reach it.
  • Zero (0.0) stays right in the middle — it becomes 0.5.

In other words, SIGMOID takes in any SUM and sends out a number that is always between 0.0 and 1.0.

SIGMOID(SUM) = 1 / (1 + e−SUM)

(Don't worry about the fancy formula — just know it's a gentle squeeze!)

INFLUENCE

When the ACTIVATION is SIGMOID, calculating the INFLUENCE of the SUM on the ACTIVATION is a bit different from ReLU — but still incredibly simple.

  • When the SUM is near 0.0: The INFLUENCE is at its highest — around 0.25. That means a small change in the SUM makes a noticeable change in the ACTIVATION.
  • When the SUM is very positive or very negative: The INFLUENCE gets closer to 0.0. That means changing the SUM barely affects the ACTIVATION — it's already squeezed to its limit!

In a NEURON, SIGMOID is gentle, smooth, and always keeps things between 0.0 and 1.0. It's like a calm teacher who never raises their voice too high or drops too low.
